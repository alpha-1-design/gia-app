# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

GIA is built local-first. This page says plainly what stays on your phone, what leaves it, and who it goes to.

## What stays on your device
Your chats, notes, settings, saved files, and memory live on your device. GIA has no account system and no GIA server that stores your conversations.

## API keys
Keys you add (for example OpenRouter) are stored on your device and sent only to the provider they belong to. We never see them.

## What leaves your device
- **AI providers.** When you send a message, the text (and any files or images you attach) goes to the provider you picked. Their privacy policy applies to that data, not ours. Choose a local model if you want nothing to leave the phone.
- **Web search and tools.** If you ask GIA to search, browse, check weather, or use a connected service (GitHub, Telegram and similar), the request goes to that service.
- **Model downloads.** Downloading a local model contacts the host that serves it.
- **Voice input.** Speech recognition on Android may be handled by Google's speech service, depending on your device. Choosing on-device (Whisper) recognition keeps audio on the phone.

## Wake word
The wake word ("Hey Jarvis") is detected on the device by a small model. The microphone is read continuously while it is on, nothing is recorded or sent anywhere, and it shows a notification while active. Only after the wake word do you speak a request, and that request follows the voice rules above. You can turn it off in Settings.

## Permissions
Microphone (voice and wake word), notifications, and storage are requested only when you use a feature that needs them. Contacts, location and files are never read unless you start an action that needs them.

## Usage statistics
Optional and off unless you opt in. They are stored on your device and are not transmitted.

## Your control
You can clear chats, memory, and keys at any time from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold none of your data, we cannot recover it for you either.

## Children
GIA is not directed at children under 13. Teachers and parents using it with students should supervise AI use.

## Changes
If this policy changes in a way that matters, GIA will ask you to read and accept it again.
