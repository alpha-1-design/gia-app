# Privacy Policy

GIA is a local-first application designed for data sovereignty and privacy.

## Data Sovereignty
GIA does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any central server. All application state and history remain on your device.

## API Keys
Your API keys (e.g., OpenRouter) are stored securely in your device's local storage or application sandbox. They are never sent to us; they are only used to authenticate requests directly to the AI service providers you choose.

## Local Processing
All interface logic, module switching, and intent analysis are performed locally on your device.

## Third-Party AI Services
When you use an AI module, your prompts are sent to the third-party provider you have configured. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of those providers (such as OpenRouter) to understand how they process your data.

## Android Permissions
The GIA Android application requires internet access to communicate with AI providers. It does not require access to your contacts, location, or local files unless explicitly requested for a specific feature you initiate.
